# Salmon Poke Bowl

Cold lunch, easy to assemble.

## Daily Meal

- 5 oz sushi-grade salmon, cubed
- 3/4 cup brown rice
- 1/2 avocado
- 1/2 cup edamame
- Soy sauce + sesame oil

| Protein | Carbs | Fat | Calories |
|---|---|---|---|
| 35g | 48g | 22g | 560 |

Toss salmon with soy + sesame oil while rice cooks.
